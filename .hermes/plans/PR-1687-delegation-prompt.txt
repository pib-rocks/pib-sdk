## Measured starting point (do not re-derive, verify against the code)
- README.md is 307 lines and already has: Architecture at a glance, Requirements, Installation, Quick start
  (Kinematics, IK, Motor control, Speech, Head camera pose, "Everything else"), Joint <-> motor names,
  Conventions, Inverse kinematics options, Using a different or updated URDF, Development, License.
- docs/REFERENCE.md is 368 lines and documents 11 modules: telemetry, backend, robot, features.drawing,
  features.poses, features.programs, features.buttons, features.camera, features.assistant, features.display,
  features.relay.
- ENTIRELY MISSING from the reference: `pib_sdk.control` (the Write motion API), `pib_sdk.kinematics`,
  `pib_sdk.robot_model`. `pib_sdk.speech` is only in the README quick start, not in the reference.
- Of 155 public API elements, 38 are mentioned nowhere. Undocumented items include:
  `ChainKinematics` (+ `joint_names`, `joint_limits_deg`, `named_configuration`, `degrees_of_freedom`),
  `ArmKinematics`, `HeadKinematics`, `ArmSide`, `ChainDefinition`, `ChainModel` (+ its members),
  `build_chain_model`, `get_chain_model`, `Personality`, `Chat`, `ChatMessage`, `AssistantState`,
  `ButtonBinding`, `ProgramSummary`, `ProgramResult`, `PoseSummary`, `apply_pose`,
  `Drawingsurface`-members (`Stroke.resampled`, `Sketch.point_count`, `DrawingSurface.point_mm`),
  `Camera.get_depth_frame`, `Camera.get_distance_at_px`, `Camera.save_depth`, `Write.send_timed_trajectory`.
- NOT documented anywhere: camera depth (get_depth_frame / get_distance_at_px / save_depth) and timed
  trajectory playback (Write.send_timed_trajectory, poses.play_pose_sequence_timed).
- One statement is FACTUALLY OUTDATED: docs/REFERENCE.md's poses section claims "there's no native
  \"pose sequence\" concept in pib-backend ... this just fetches and applies each pose in order".
  That is wrong now: pib-backend has a software trajectory controller (PR-1660, monotone cubic Hermite
  interpolation, blends through via-points) and the SDK exposes timed sequences (PR-1685 / pib-sdk 0.5.1).
- There are NO tutorials and NO examples anywhere in the repo.
